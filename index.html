🚤 Hoverboard Design

Explore the design and concept of our innovative hoverboard. This section presents the main design of the hoverboard and its overall structure.

⚙️ Structural & Mechanical Features

An overview of the main structural and mechanical elements incorporated into the Special K hoverboard design.

01

Frame & Chassis

The main frame is made of styrofoam, providing benefits in light weight, low density, and impact absorbtion. Minor details ara made of wood and steel. The inflated air bed is made of thin durable polyethylene.

02

Propulsion System

Two motors spin the ventilators generate force for the hoverboard to move. One is pushing it forward, another upwards, making it nearly float over the water surface.

03

Power System

The power for motors and steering control system is being provided from an AC battery through wires.

04

Stability & Control

Control of the steering is being made distantly using a controller connected to RC control unit, that is moving a flap behind the top ventilator, making the hoverboard turn to different sides. Dynamic exterior design provides stability in motion.

05

Dynamics

Structure of a hoverboard is designed to be optimal in terms of aerodynamics and weight distribution, resulting in perfect performance in action.

06

Safety

Structural and mechanical considerations intended to improve reliability and user safety.
